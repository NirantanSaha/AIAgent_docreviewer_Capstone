Precedent 1: Schrems II Transfer Risk
Cross-border transfers require a documented safeguard, and contracts that name the Standard Contractual Clauses generally reduce immediate transfer-risk ambiguity, though operational measures may still require review.

Precedent 2: Audit Right Narrowness
Enterprise customers often expect a mix of periodic audit rights and event-triggered review rights. A single annual audit right can be workable, but narrow notice windows and limited scopes may still create negotiation friction.

Precedent 3: Liability Cap Mismatch
When privacy obligations are broad but the liability cap is tied only to recent service fees, legal teams often flag whether the cap is proportionate to regulatory and incident-response exposure.

Precedent 4: Subprocessor Objection Model
An opt-out or deemed-approved model for new subprocessors is common in cloud agreements, but privacy teams may ask for stronger objection rights or termination rights when objections cannot be resolved.

Precedent 5: Deletion Window Clarity
Explicit deletion periods after termination usually reduce operational ambiguity. Missing or overly broad deletion timelines often trigger follow-up review.
